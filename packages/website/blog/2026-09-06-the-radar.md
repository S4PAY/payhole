PayHole blocks a name on every phone and browser running it the moment the network learns about
it. Until now the learning was invisible: a node knew, the swarm knew, and nobody could see it
happen. The radar makes it visible.

## What it shows

- **Swarm confirmations.** How many names the swarm confirmed in the last 24 hours and the last
  7 days, the newest twenty with their category and how many reporters agreed, and how many are
  flagged and still waiting for enough agreement.
- **List growth.** Each subscribed list with the names it gained and lost in the last 24 hours and
  a sample of the new ones. On the public resolver that is ScamSniffer, Phishing.Database, and
  StevenBlack, about 815,000 names in all.
- **New names by kind.** Drainer infrastructure, wallet drainers, phishing, counterfeit token
  sites, trackers, ads.
- **Brands impersonated.** The brands the new names trade on, most often first, with examples.
  `metamask-verify` on a free hosting platform counts for MetaMask; `metamask.io` does not, because
  a brand's own name is never impersonation.

## Where it lives

- [payhole.org/radar.html](/radar.html), which reads the endpoint from the page.
- The Radar tab in [PayHole for Android 0.3](/downloads/payhole.apk), which replaces the Lists tab
  and keeps the list cards underneath.
- `https://dns.payhole.org/radar`, JSON any wallet, exchange, or dashboard may read. It is
  rebuilt once a minute, cacheable for a minute, open to any origin, and shares the resolver's rate
  limit. Every Sinkhole node serves its own at `/api/radar`.

## Where it does not come from

The public resolver keeps no query log, and the radar does not need one. It is built from two
things a node already has: the flags it received from the swarm and the lists it refreshes. Nodes
now remember the last 500 confirmations and the last twelve changing refreshes per list, with the
names each one gained. A refresh with no earlier copy of the list to compare against records
nothing, so a fresh node shows quiet lists until its second refresh. The public node was updated
today, so its first day will fill in as the lists move.

## What comes next

The same snapshot, sold over x402 to wallets and exchanges as a feed, paid in USDG, with the
operators' share going to the nodes that reported and the protocol's share buying and burning the
token. The radar you can read for free is the product; the feed is the same product with a
receipt.
