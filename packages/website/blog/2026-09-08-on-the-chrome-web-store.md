Submitted yesterday afternoon, live this morning: the shield and the wallet guard, as reviewed by
Google, are on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/calplcemfhaiomnhjphgmicdamdjhhep).
One click in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera, or Vivaldi, and updates arrive on their own from now on.

Nothing changed in the extension itself since [0.4](/blog/extension-0-4-ready-for-the-store/). What
changed is the door. The [extension page](/extension.html) now leads with the store and keeps the zip
for anyone who prefers to load it by hand, or runs a browser the store does not reach.

The store copy of 0.1, the spending pocket, is gone; anyone who installed it gets 0.4 on the next
update check, with the pocket switched off and waiting behind its switch in the Shield tab.
