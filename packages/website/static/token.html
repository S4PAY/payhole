The token pays no one. It is bought and burned.

No staking. No yield. No cashback. No airdrop. Fixed supply. The only thing burning does is raise your tier.

Token address: not set yet. The Safe sets it once after the launch.

Burn mechanics

burnWith(usdg or eth)

Burn with USDG or ETH

Send USDG or ETH to the vault. It buys PAYHOLE through the pool and burns it in the same transaction. You never hold the token. The extension routes its top-up fee here.

burnDirect(amount)

Burn what you hold

Already hold PAYHOLE. Approve the vault and burn it. Gone in one transaction.

unlock(tier)

Unlock a tier

Burn the tier's price and the vault records the tier for your address. Upgrades only, full price each time. The extension raises your limits from that record.

Tiers

Prices are set in BurnVault by the Safe and read live from chain. Limits are enforced by the extension.
