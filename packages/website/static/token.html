The token pays no one. It is bought and burned.
No staking. No yield. No cashback. No airdrop. Fixed supply. The only thing burning does is raise your tier.
Burn mechanics
burnWith(usdg)
Burn with USDG
Send USDG to BurnVault. The vault buys PAYHOLE at market and burns it. You never hold the token.
burnDirect(amount)
Burn what you hold
Already hold PAYHOLE. Send it to the vault. It is burned in the same transaction.
unlock(pocket)
Unlock a tier
Burns are credited to the pocket that sent them. Cross a threshold and the pocket's limits rise. That is all.

No payout
Holding PAYHOLE earns nothing. Burning it raises your tier. There is no other function.
Bounties for reports

Anyone with the app can report a name the network does not block yet. When the report was first and the network later agrees, it pays, in USDG, from the protocol's fees. The token is never the payment; holding it is part of the ticket.

Per confirmed first report
0.50 USDG
for a wallet drainer or drainer infrastructure.
0.30 USDG
for phishing or a counterfeit token site. Trackers and ads earn nothing.
What counts
First reporter only; a name already blocked or on a list is never counted. Confirmed by two tier holders other than the reporter, caught by a public list within fourteen days, or confirmed by PayHole's own review of the evidence, which also adds the name to the PayHole list. Ten paid reports per wallet per day. Names later allowlisted pay nothing. The node gathers evidence on every report and remembers who confirmed what, so nobody confirms their own.
Getting paid
Payouts are requested from the app once a wallet is owed 10 USDG, and go to a wallet that holds a tier or at least ten dollars of PAYHOLE at the moment of payout, priced from the chain: the Pons curve until the pool graduates, the pool after. The node keeps the ledger; the project sends the USDG and marks it paid.

Report from the Check tab of the Android app, add a rewards wallet under Your reporter key, and Your reports shows what every report earned.
