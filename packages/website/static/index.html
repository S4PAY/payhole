Questions
What happens when a site goes over its cap?
You get one prompt. Approve, raise the cap, or decline. Nothing moves until you answer.
Can a site see my other addresses?
No. Each address is derived for one origin. A site sees its own address and its own cap.
Who pays gas?
Not you. x402 settlement is gasless for the payer. The facilitator submits the transaction.
Does the token earn anything?
No. It is bought and burned. There is no staking, yield, cashback, or airdrop.
Is Sinkhole a VPN?
No. It is a DNS resolver you run yourself. Blocked domains get no answer. Your traffic goes where it always went.
Can I read the contracts?
Yes. They are open source, tested, and verified on Sourcify. Read them before you fund a pocket.
