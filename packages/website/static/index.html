Everything the pocket does

Capped · Automatic · Private · Fair · Burned

Caps

One number per site. Nothing above it moves without you.

Silent payments

HTTP 402 settled in USDG. Gasless for you. No popups under the cap.

Per-site addresses

Derived from your seed, one per origin. A site sees one address and nothing else.

Agent session keys

Give a tool or agent its own key with its own cap. Revoke it any time, or all at once.

Creator tips

Registered domains get tipped per visit from your pocket. Once a day by default, or off.

Sinkhole

Self-hosted DNS. Blocks trackers and drainers. Shares scam flags in a peer swarm.

Unlock tiers

Burn to raise your limits. Two tiers above the default. Nothing else changes.

Token burns

Fixed supply. Only ever bought and burned. Pays no one.

Questions

What happens when a site goes over its cap?
You get one prompt. Approve this payment or decline. Caps change in the dashboard, never from a prompt.
Can a site see my other addresses?
No. Each address is derived for one origin. A site sees its own address and what that address paid it.
Who pays gas?
x402 settlement is gasless for the payer; the facilitator submits it. Moving USDG from your pocket to a per-site address is a small transaction paid by your owner address.
Does the token earn anything?
No. It is bought and burned. There is no staking, yield, cashback, or airdrop.
Is Sinkhole a VPN?
No. It is a DNS resolver you run yourself. Blocked domains get no answer. Your traffic goes where it always went.
Can I read the contracts?
Yes. They are open source, tested, verified on Sourcify, and not audited. Read them before you fund a pocket.
