Register your domain. Get tipped per visit.

No platform, no account, no cut. Prove you own the domain and visitors' pockets tip your wallet in USDG.

01

Add a TXT record

At your DNS provider, add one record that names your wallet.

_payhole.example.com  TXT  "payhole=0xYourWallet"
02

Request the attestation

The verifier reads the record and signs a statement binding the domain to your wallet. It is valid for one hour.

03

Submit the claim

Any wallet sends the attestation to CreatorRegistry. From a browser wallet here, or with the calldata shown below.

04

Get tipped per visit

Each visit from a PayHole user tips your wallet from their pocket, once a day by default. It shows on the explorer.
