Register your domain. Get tipped per visit.
No platform, no account, no cut. Prove you own the domain and visitors' pockets tip your wallet in USDG.
01
Add a TXT record
At your DNS provider, add one record that names your wallet.
_payhole.example.com  TXT  "payhole=0xYourWallet"
02
Sign an attestation
Sign a message from that wallet. It binds the domain to the address.
03
Verifier checks both
verifier.payhole.org reads the record, checks the signature, and writes you to CreatorRegistry.
04
Get tipped per visit
Each visit from a PayHole user tips your wallet from their pocket. It shows on the explorer.
