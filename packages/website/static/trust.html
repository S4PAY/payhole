Here is every part that touches your money, what each one can see, and where to read the code.

Architecture

Your machine

Extension

Manifest V3. Holds the encrypted seed. Sees every 402.

Sinkhole

Self-hosted DNS. Blocks trackers and drainers. Peer swarm for scam flags.

Derived

Per-site addresses

One per origin, from the seed. Each with its own cap.

BudgetAccount

Your pocket. Enforces caps on chain. Created by BudgetAccountFactory. Only you can move its funds.

Settlement

Facilitator

Submits x402 settlements and pays gas. Can only settle what a per-site address signed.

Burn vault

Buys and burns PAYHOLE. Records tiers for addresses that burn through unlock.

Creators

Verifier

payhole.org/api. Reads TXT records and signs attestations. Holds no funds.

Registry

CreatorRegistry. Domain to wallet. Tips go straight to the wallet.
