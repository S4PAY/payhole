Get the extension
Extension
Install PayHole and fund your first pocket.

The extension is a capped USDG pocket for Chrome and other Chromium browsers: Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi. Until the Chrome Web Store listing is approved it installs from a zip in about two minutes. This page walks through everything from the download to your first paid page, with the real screens.

Download payhole-extension.zipTry a paid page
Version 0.1.1. Manifest V3. No account, no sign-up, nothing leaves your device except the payments you make.
1. Install it

Two minutes. The only unusual step is loading the folder instead of clicking an install button, which is how every extension outside the store is installed.

01
Download and extract
Download the zip and extract it into a folder you will keep, for example Documents/payhole-extension. On Windows right-click the zip and choose Extract All; on a Mac double-click it. The browser loads the extension from this folder on every start, so do not delete it afterwards. Inside you should see manifest.json and a few folders.
02
Load it unpacked
Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, brave://extensions in Brave, or edge://extensions in Edge. Turn on Developer mode at the top right, click Load unpacked, and pick the folder you extracted. Do not pick a subfolder. PayHole appears in the list with its green vortex icon.
03
Pin it
Click the puzzle icon right of the address bar and pin PayHole so the icon stays visible. Later, to update, download the zip again, extract it over the same folder, and click the small refresh arrow on the PayHole card in chrome://extensions. Your seed, pocket, and settings stay.
2. Create the vault

The vault is an encrypted seed that lives only in your browser. Every address PayHole uses is derived from it: the owner address that controls the pocket, one address per site, and the session keys for agents.

04
Create a seed
Click the PayHole icon. Choose Create seed, enter a password twice, and click Create. The recovery phrase is shown once. Write it down on paper and keep it somewhere safe: anyone with the phrase controls every address of this wallet, and without it a lost browser means lost access. Click I wrote it down when you have.
Already have a PayHole seed from another browser? Choose Import seed instead and paste the phrase.
05
Know the two buttons
The popup has Dashboard, which opens the full control panel in a tab, and Lock, which locks the vault until you enter the password again. Locked means no payments.
3. Fund the pocket

Money lives in two places: a little on the owner address, which signs transactions and pays gas, and the rest in the pocket, a BudgetAccount contract on Robinhood Chain that only your owner key controls.

06
Copy the owner address
Open the Dashboard. The Budget tab shows Owner with a Copy button, plus Owner ETH and Owner USDG. Send to that address on Robinhood Chain, chain id 4663: some USDG to start with, 1 to 5 is plenty, and a little ETH for gas, 0.001 ETH covers many transactions on this chain. Until the ETH arrives the panel says "needs a little ETH for gas".
07
Create the account
When both balances show, click Create account. That is one transaction from the owner. The BudgetAccount line changes from "not created" to the pocket's address; copy it, because sending USDG straight to that address also counts as a deposit.
08
Top up
In the Top up panel enter an amount, for example 5, and click Top up. Two transactions move USDG from the owner into the pocket: an approval and the deposit. The green line reports what was deposited. A note about a skipped fee is normal until the token's swap route exists; the fee is what buys and burns PAYHOLE on every top-up once it does.
09
Read the numbers
Account USDG is what the pocket holds. Agent global cap is the shared limit for agent keys, 25 USDG at tier zero. Site payments shows how much sites were paid in total and today, and the prompt threshold: above 25 USDG in a day the extension asks before paying.
4. Pay for a page

You do not have to hunt for a site that charges. There is one on this website.

10
Load the article
Open payhole.org/try.html and click Load the article. The page asks for 0.01 USDG with a 402. The first time you pay a site, the owner sends two transactions: one sets the site's cap on chain, one funds the site's own address with a small chunk from the pocket, 0.5 USDG by default. Then the extension signs the payment, the facilitator settles it on chain, and the article appears with the settlement transaction under it. Allow a few seconds the first time. The second load pays instantly, because the site's address still holds the rest of the chunk.
11
Look at the popup
Click the icon while on that page. This site shows the origin, the address that pays it, and Spent so far against its cap. Payments lists every payment with its status and the settlement transaction; settled means the money moved.
5. Control spending

Every limit is yours, and the important ones are enforced by the contract, not by the extension.

12
Per-site caps
Every site gets a cap, 1 USDG by default. The Sites tab lists every site that was ever paid with its spend, cap, payment count, and last payment. Change a cap and Save, or Block a site so it is never paid again. Set a cap for a site before you visit it by typing its address.
13
The prompt
A price above a site's cap, or a day's total above the threshold, opens the approval page instead of paying: the site, the resource, the amount, who gets paid, and what is left of each cap. Pay once approves that one request without changing any cap. Deny refuses it.
14
Pause, withdraw, lock
Pause all payments in the popup stops every payment until you untick it. Withdraw in the Budget tab moves USDG from the pocket back to the owner, or to any address you type. Lock in the popup locks the vault.
6. Beyond the basics
Agents
Session keys for tools
The Agents tab creates a named key with its own cap and hands you an environment block for the payhole command line. A script pays APIs from the same pocket, never above its cap, never holding your seed.
Tips
Pay creators per visit
Off by default. Turn it on in the Tips tab and registered sites receive a small tip once per domain per day, from a small float. The Registry tab is where a site owner claims their own domain.
Blocklist
Drainers and trackers
Flag a hostname, import lists, and sync with a Sinkhole node on your network by entering its address and token. Blocked domains never connect, on any site.
Tiers
Burn to unlock
The Tiers tab shows what each tier unlocks: bigger pockets, more agent keys, reporting rights in the swarm. Unlocking burns PAYHOLE from your owner address through the BurnVault.
Settings
The chain and the defaults
The RPC endpoint, the contract addresses, the default site cap, the top-up chunk, and the prompt threshold. Reset clears local data; Remove seed deletes the vault from this browser.
Recovery
If the browser is lost
Install PayHole anywhere, choose Import seed, enter the phrase. The pocket and every address come back, because they are derived from the seed and the money never left the chain.
Good to know

The seed never leaves the extension, and PayHole has no server that sees it. Payments are signed transfer authorizations; the site or its facilitator settles them, and only the amount asked, within your caps.

A site learns exactly one thing about you: the address that pays it. Not your balance, not your other addresses, not what you paid elsewhere.

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in that browser. The pocket and its balance stay on chain under your seed; re-import the seed to reach them again.

Questions about what the extension can and cannot do are answered on the Trust page, and what it stores and sends is on the privacy page.
