Buy a tier. The vault burns for you.

A tier is a wallet's standing in the network: its flags count in the swarm, its node may report, and its linked phones report with weight. It costs USDG, 10, 50, or 250. The BurnVault takes the USDG, buys PAYHOLE with it, and burns what it bought. Nobody is paid. Two signatures from your wallet: one approval, one unlock. No node, no command line.

What a tier gives
01
Reports that count
Flags from a tier holder's node, or from a phone linked to the wallet on the link page, count toward confirmation on every node and take the fast lane when a list already names the domain.
02
Bounties paid out
A wallet that holds a tier, or enough PAYHOLE, can be paid the bounties its confirmed first reports earn. The rules are on the token page.
03
Where the USDG goes
Into the vault, which buys PAYHOLE and burns it. Until the token's pool graduates, the vault holds the USDG and burns as soon as the route exists. Either way it never comes back out as a payout, and the token never does.
